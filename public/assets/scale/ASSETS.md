# Assets for the `scale` chapter

Everything here was generated locally with `tools/gen` and is a placeholder the owner can replace file by file.
Sources, sidecars and the scripts that made them (`gen1.sh`, `gen2.sh`, `gen3.sh`) are in `tools/gen/src/scale/`.
No words, numbers or UI are baked into any image: storefront screens, the toggle board, the monitor, labels and the product sheet are drawn in canvas or DOM.

The object boxes used by the labelling bench (`SAMPLES[].box` in `src/chapters/scale/data.ts`) are measured from BiRefNet cut-outs of the stream images (`tools/gen/src/scale/boxes.json`); replace an image and its box together.
The regions vision "reads" on each product photo (`PRODUCTS[].regions`) were placed by eye for these photos; replace a photo and its regions together.

GLB props are served compressed (`npx gltf-transform optimize in out --compress meshopt --texture-compress webp --texture-size 1024 --simplify false --palette false --join false --flatten false --instance false --prune-solid-textures false`); the loader decodes them with MeshoptDecoder. Uncompressed originals: `tools/gen/backup-glb/`.
