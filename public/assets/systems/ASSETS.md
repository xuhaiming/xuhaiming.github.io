# systems: generated placeholder assets

Everything here was made locally with `tools/gen` (ComfyUI: Z-Image Turbo, Trellis 2, MiniMax H3; Blender for GLB clean-up).
All of it is placeholder art the owner can replace file by file; names and sizes should stay the same.
Raw generations, sidecar JSON (exact prompt, seed, model) and the two scripts that made them (`gen_images.sh`, `gen_assets.sh`) are in `tools/gen/src/systems/`.

No UI, words or numbers are baked into any image: those are drawn in DOM and canvas (`src/chapters/systems/apps.ts`).

`loop-*.jpg` are the poster frames written by `web_video.py`.

GLB props are served compressed (`npx gltf-transform optimize in out --compress meshopt --texture-compress webp --texture-size 1024 --simplify false --palette false --join false --flatten false --instance false --prune-solid-textures false`); the loader decodes them with MeshoptDecoder. Uncompressed originals: `tools/gen/backup-glb/`.
